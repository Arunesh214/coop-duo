Duo Dash

Two players, one screen, zero solo heroics. Step on glowing plates together to open doors, use each other as platforms to reach high ledges, and both of you must reach the green exit to clear a level.

Player 1 (red) A left   D right   W jump
Player 2 (cyan) ← left   → right   ↑ jump
Press R anytime to restart the current level. Jump on top of your partner to reach higher ground.
